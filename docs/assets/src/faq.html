FAQ

Questions people ask.

No

Does it send my clipboard anywhere?

Never. It stays in a local database. Only the update check talks to GitHub.

Safe

What about passwords?

Password managers are skipped. Copied passwords, keys and card numbers are hidden and deleted after 5 minutes.

No

Will it slow my computer down?

The recorder uses about 25 MB. The panel frees its memory a few minutes after you close it.

Once

Why does Windows warn me?

Clipio is new and not signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only the first time.

Yes

Does it work on Wayland?

Yes. Bind clipio toggle to a key. On GNOME it copies and you paste.

Free

What does it cost?

Nothing. MIT license. No account, no ads, no trial.
