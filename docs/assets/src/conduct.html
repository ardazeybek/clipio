Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here.

Whatever your experience, background or identity. Three promises keep it that way.

Be kind and patient

Especially with newcomers. Every expert once asked the “obvious” question.

Ideas, not people

Argue about the code all you like. No insults, harassment or personal attacks.

Assume good intent

Ask before you judge. Most rough messages were just written in a hurry.

Something went wrong? Tell the maintainer by email. Comments, issues or pull requests that break these promises may be edited or removed. Repeat offenders may be blocked.
